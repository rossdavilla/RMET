Reading the Mind in the Eyes
Short Form

Instructions: You will see 18 photographs of the eye region of a face. For each one, choose which of the four words best describes what the person is thinking or feeling. You may feel that more than one word fits — choose the one you consider most suitable. Read all four words before choosing. Work quickly, but you are not timed.
18 photographs · about 5 minutes
Baron-Cohen, Wheelwright, Hill, Raste & Plumb (2001). Free for academic, non-commercial use.
Example
The four choices here are jealous, panicked, arrogant and hateful.
The correct answer is panicked.
